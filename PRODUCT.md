# AI Trend Agent

## What it is

An agent that watches the AI ecosystem, verifies what it finds against primary sources, and tells a curriculum owner which parts of the SDA Agentic AI Bootcamp need updating.

## The mechanism that makes it different

Competing trend trackers collect and summarise. This one **refuses to confirm what it cannot check**. Every claim carries the document it was read from, and a claim is only confirmed when an official release says the same thing. The system distinguishes three states and shows them plainly: an independent cross-source check, a primary report (the release speaking about itself), and unverified.

## Who reads the output

The curriculum owner of a bootcamp cohort, and the judging panel at the capstone. Neither reads JSON. Both ask the same question in different words: *what changed this week, and does our course need to change because of it?*

## The scene

A weekly review, ten minutes, on a laptop. The reader wants the shortlist first, the reasoning on demand, and no claim they cannot trace back to a URL.

## Truths the interface must carry

- 400 signals a run; most are discussion that names nothing checkable. Saying so is part of the product.
- Zero cross-source confirmations in the current data window — because forum posts rarely state versions. This is measured, not hidden.
- Four tracked packages are not covered by the curriculum at all. Those gaps are the most actionable output.
- A recommendation without a traceable reason is worthless here.

## Surfaces

`site.html` — a single offline page generated from one saved run. Four sections: what to do, curriculum map, evidence, and an honest account of the run itself. Bilingual Arabic/English with a toggle, because the panel is Saudi and the data is English.

## Constraints

- No server, no network at view time. It must open from a file on any laptop.
- Generated by `python -m web.site` from artifacts on disk; the page never computes judgements of its own.
- Light theme, SDA identity.

## Assumptions (from the brief, not an interview)

- Team lead specified: four sections, SDA colours, light theme, product name "AI Trend Agent", bilingual with a toggle.
- The page chrome is monochrome: navy-black ink #15172b on cool paper #f4f5f7, one radius scale,
  no accent colour of its own. Colour on this page always means something.
- Four reserved status colours, validated for colour-blind separation before shipping:
  #1a7f47 confirmed by an independent source, #1f6fb2 the release reporting itself,
  #8a7c0f unverified or drifting, #bd2b52 an API a newer release removed. Text uses a darker
  step of the same hue so every label clears WCAG AA.
